      Dates. City dates come from the Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) observation trees once recorded, otherwise from the Japan Meteorological Corporation (JMC) forecast. Spot dates are JMC forecasts. Averages are the normal dates published with the forecast. For autumn leaves, JMC gives only the difference from normal.

      Shading. Colour between spots is interpolated from the six nearest spots and cities. It fades out 28 km from the nearest one, so open sea and empty mountains stay plain. Each tree grove stands on a real JMC spot.

      Front speed is how fast the interpolated arrival line moves, averaged over a week.

      Terrain. Terrain Tiles on AWS (Mapzen, sources). Map drawn with MapLibre GL JS.
